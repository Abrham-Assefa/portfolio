01 / About

From research notebooks to real products.

I started as a Full-Stack Developer at Ethiopia's Information Network Security Agency (INSA), where I spent two years building and shipping web and mobile applications in production — real users, real security constraints, real deadlines. That experience is where "elegant solutions to complex problems" stopped being a tagline and became a habit.

I'm now channeling that into an MSc in Computer Science with Artificial Intelligence at the University of Verona, working across Computer Vision & Deep Learning, Machine Learning & Pattern Recognition, and AI & Finance — with the same end-to-end mindset: data, model, API, interface. I care about explainability (Grad-CAM, Integrated Gradients, attention maps) as much as accuracy — a model nobody can trust doesn't ship.

Based in Verona, Italy, and open to developer, AI engineering, and applied ML roles — remote or on-site.
