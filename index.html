01 / About

From research notebooks to real products.

I started as a Full-Stack Developer at Ethiopia's Information Network Security Agency (INSA), where I spent two years building and shipping web and mobile applications in production — real users, real security constraints, real deadlines. Based in Verona, Italy, and open to developer, AI engineering, and applied ML roles — remote or on-site.
